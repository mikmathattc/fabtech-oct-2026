Calculation Assumptions

  • 50 operating weeks per year and 8 hours per shift.
  • A full-time welder equivalent is 2,000 paid hours per year.
  • Labor cost is the production time consumed by each tip change, valued at the hourly labor cost entered.
  • Tip cost uses the purchase price per tip entered.

These figures are estimates based on the information entered. They describe the current operation only and are not a guarantee of any future result.
